## The distillation

The task is to maintain a usable understanding across a gap. Five connected ideas do most of the work:

1. **Read the evidence before choosing storage.** A mixed exchange can disclose a board's location, settle a booking, reject a proposal and refine an understanding of a person. “Worth remembering” cannot start after those discoveries have already been filtered out. Both voices and action evidence contribute; an adopted plan remains a plan.
2. **Different kinds of knowledge need different support.** A stated fact survives its first disclosure. A person's explicit distinction differs from an inferred rhythm. A useful interpretation keeps its scope, alternatives and grounding facts. Uncertainty about meaning is not permission to discard evidence.
3. **Memory units follow retrieval purpose.** Detail and meaning may each deserve a node because different questions find them. A plain fact needs no invented principle; a sprawling synthesis must not swallow independent facts. A specific edge makes a relation available to a future walk.
4. **Maintenance follows claims, not topics.** One event can falsify a title, content, future trigger, question, evidence statement, type or edge description across multiple nodes. Revise the affected claims while carrying still-true details forward. A fetched id is prior knowledge, not permission to mint a lookalike.
5. **Completion follows observed results.** Working lists expose decisions; returned reads and writes establish what actually happened. Facts, thoughts and unresolved questions have different durable homes. A prior no-mint verdict is not future evidence or policy.

The unchanged opening keeps ownership, generosity, detail plus meaning and the value of honest edges. The shorter middle explains the discriminators rather than repeatedly warning about the same failure. The untouched gist still activates those discriminators at the current input. No new list, scratchpad phase, mandatory field, trace quota, inference threshold or tool restriction was introduced.

### Source refs are not a percentage target

The principle is selective correlation and episodic visibility: when does this memory need the scene beside it? The board and measured queue finding stand without refs. The correction and identity scenes can earn dense refs locally. Preserve the generating 1–3 turns when warranted, not all nodes or a fixed proportion. All original worked source-ref fields are unchanged. Neither zero-everywhere nor 100%-coverage is an intended success criterion; evaluation needs scene-appropriate selection.

## Boundaries and possible next options

No shared trace-contract closure, catalog/residue rendering, runner continuation or confidence representation was changed. Tool availability remains intact, including tools beyond the three emphasized acts. Tom-approved `$defs` schema reuse is a separate input-size lever; removing tools is a different, unapproved decision and is not a component of any template here.

A further aggressive option would replace several identity examples with one cross-session identity episode. I did **not** author that fourth candidate: removing repeated examples would also change the distribution of hot register, both voices, source refs, concrete identity tokens and rule tension. The current three offer cleaner comparisons without pretending a single “representative” scene covers those dimensions.

No claim of behavioral parity, field-rate parity, lower API cost or recall improvement follows from the static checks. Before promotion, actual composed requests must be inspected and the approved evaluation must test fresh mixed and sequential cases, preservation of still-true details, useful and scoped thoughts, and scene-selective provenance alongside the existing factual-repair measures. Frozen V2 remains a separate baseline.

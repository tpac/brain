## What the round found beyond its question

1. **The guard's condition becomes the encoder's claim.** The advice carrier scopes a method node of mine to "a
   threshold I delivered AND they took up". The arm minted fewer such nodes and wrote "took up / adopted" into 40% of
   the ones it kept (baseline 16%). A scope condition phrased as a fact about the person invites the encoder to assert
   that fact. The next carrier of this kind should depict the boundary by what is *absent* (no number, no uptake) and
   keep the licence for the node on the person's side — "their pick, on their node" — rather than on a claim the encoder
   must make.
2. **The zero-write window is not closed.** One first window in 45 for the quote arm ended with lists and no tool call —
   "nothing here warrants a write" over a window holding a location, three plans and a preference. The thin worked
   window is in every arm; the class recurs at a low rate on the same input shape (a cold-start advice Q&A).
3. **A teardown crash is not a failed repetition.** The launcher treats any nonzero exit as failure and kills the
   siblings; a process that has written every `final_brain.json` and then dies in onnxruntime teardown cost the baseline
   a sequence and a resume. Completion should be read from the records.
4. **The blind read is relative.** The same `v3_6_full` template scored 73 of 96 beside `v3_4_live` (V3.6 cell) and 55
   of 96 beside two arms built on it. A pack's sum compares its members; the number travels only with its pack.
5. **Downstream and the source can disagree.** The quote arm's two hits on gpt4_e414231f were judged correct against the
   LongMemEval gold from a node whose "installed" claim the blind reviewer of the same pack called unsupported by the
   source. The gold rewards the answer; the reviewer grades the evidence. Both are kept.
6. **The method nodes were hubs.** Removing 26 of them took 37 edges with them and left ten more of the person's
   facts as isolates. A guard that trims a node type needs a readout on what the trimmed nodes were holding together.

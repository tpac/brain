## Reading

- **The weaves that transferred.** *Question lane:* the live tool text ("skip when the
  title already asks it") pulled V3.3's question fill down to 27% on fresh material;
  V3.4 recovers it to 42% (production 40%), to 45% on the regression set and 90% on the
  design source. *Thought shape:* every V3.4 thought is a hunch or a connection — the
  swallowed-exception hazard, the October trip outside Shark's Cove's calm season, the
  reader's narrowing funnel — where V3.3's were epistemic caveats; counts stay small
  (2 / 6 / 1 across the three cells against production's 14 / 14 / 9). The measured
  driver is the example shape, not the tool description: V3.3's prompt on the live tools
  still wrote 2 thoughts in 182 nodes. *Structure:* on the regression set V3.4 has the
  fewest near-twins (1) and fewer isolates than V3.3 (5 against 9), more relations and
  verbs, and the most `connect_to` on revise (12 ops, 10 of 13 edges landed with their
  why; the misses are two sibling titles passed as `target`). *event_time:* 81% fresh,
  100% on the design source, the highest in every cell.
- **The weaves that did not, or not yet.** *Quote moves with the fact:* V3.4 refreshed the
  quote in 1 of 3 repeats on the film-count corpus (V3.3 0 of 3) and touched
  `their_raw_quote` in 0 of 30 fresh revisions. The example shows it; the encoder rarely
  does it. *Coverage:* V3.4 is still the smallest memory in every cell (159 fresh nodes
  against 182 and 211; 139 regression against 148), lowest where the source is an
  assistant-led technical exchange — the debugging synthetic yields 2 nodes in two of
  three repeats where production yields 5–6, because V3.4 folds the lesson and the
  mechanism into the bug node instead of minting them. The Skip-sentence weave did not
  reach that shape; the Kauai window created 2 nodes in one regression repeat (V3.3: 0
  in one). *Refused-op state:* V3.4 had one refusal (a no-op swap); the class it was
  written for did not recur, so it is unmeasured.
- **Downstream.** Fresh: V3.4 and V3.3-on-live-tools 12 correct of 15 each, production 9.
  Regression: V3.4 12 of 15 against V3.3's 14 — two museum-count misses where the Art
  Cube date is held but not in the retrieved five, and one destination miss in the
  4-node Kauai/Oahu sequence. Both candidate arms beat production on the fresh material;
  the weave did not move the answer test.
- **A finding the fresh items produced on their own.** The tennis item restates a habit:
  weekly in March, every other week in July; the question asks for both. All nine
  sequences of all three arms read July as a correction of March and kept one value; no
  arm held two dated values. The semantic-fidelity challenge gains a row for it (a
  restated fact that differs: correction of the record, or change in the world?).
- **Infrastructure.** The write path is clean in every one of 112 fresh and 36 regression
  revise ops; the one hard failure is the dispatcher crash on a string revision item
  (id:2c9cccb8), a pre-write type check the batch handlers lack for `revise_batch` and
  `remember_batch`.
- **Overfitting guard, read back.** The two weaves that were authored against a class
  (question fill, thought shape) moved on unseen material; the two authored against a
  specific loss (the film-count quote, the Kauai gate) barely moved there. That is the
  expected signature of shape-level weaving versus fixing an instance, and it is what the
  fresh corpora were for.
- **Blind read, 12 packs.** Production 110, V3.4 108, V3.3 on live tools 94 of 168; a
  level sum over opposite profiles — V3.4 takes evidence, ownership and scope 21 to 4,
  production takes voice and synthesis 21 to 11. Detail at the end of this document.
- **Position.** V3.4 is a modest, real improvement on V3.3 on the surfaces a live turn
  reads — question and event_time lanes, structure, thought shape — and neutral on
  answers; it does not close the coverage gap to production, which now looks like a
  stance (fold into one node) rather than a gate. The tools finding stands on its own: any
  V3.x that ships runs on the live schemas, and the live `question` description costs a
  lane that the prompt then has to buy back. Nothing is merged or deployed.

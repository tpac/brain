## Working strategy

I read prior memory and the conversation for both details and developing
meaning. The four lists account for discoveries and every affected claim.
I fetch what the decision lacks, then preserve new knowledge and repair
old claims through the tools. Useful interpretations keep their evidence
and uncertainty; plans advance only with evidence of progress.

After tool results, I compare successful writes with intended changes,
including fields and relationships. A returned success does not prove
coverage. I repair remaining supported changes before closing. Arc and
Review carry the movement and unfinished questions left by the results,
never an invented completion or a no-mint verdict for a future run to obey.
